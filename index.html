Profile

Aboul-Karim Mohamed El Maarouf is a high-performance computing research engineer at IFP Energies nouvelles (IFPEN). He works on speeding up the sparse linear solvers at the core of porous media flow simulations, used in particular for geological CO₂ storage.

During his PhD at the University of Bordeaux, carried out jointly by IFPEN and Inria, he developed two methods:

  • a graph-coloring matrix ordering (ColorRCM) that enables fine-grained parallelism in the triangular solves arising from ILU(0) incomplete factorization, without degrading the convergence of Krylov solvers;
  • a synchronization barrier combined with a reduction, up to four times faster than the OpenMP reduction on multi-core processors.

Both methods are now part of MCGSolver, IFPEN's solver library. He now contributes to the Alien ecosystem, a framework for linear solver libraries co-developed by IFPEN and CEA.
